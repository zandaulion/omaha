🔒 AI analysis & your notes

Gemini always receives the company's filed financials, computed ratios and the 12-point checklist. It never receives your journal entries.

That means your conviction rating, target buy price, core rationale and sell guardrails — so the analysis can argue with your reasoning instead of ignoring it. Off by default. It applies from the next analysis you run, not to ones already saved.

📋 What this app is

Every score here is arithmetic run over filed company statements. The same company produces the same 0–100 score for everyone using the app — nothing is tailored to you, your finances or your goals. Where the filings do not contain a number, the app reports it as not measured rather than estimating one.

Your reasons, target price and reconsideration flags are your own writing, saved in Omaha. The app records them and reminds you of them. It does not endorse them.

This is not investment advice, and nothing in it is a recommendation to buy or sell. A DCF fair value is the output of assumptions you choose — move a slider and the "fair value" moves with it. AI analysis is generated text and can be wrong, confidently and in detail. Your investment decisions remain yours.

💾 Backup & Restore

Download your reasons, conditions, dated reviews, and journal notes — or restore them from a file. Restoring merges: the newer version of each thesis wins, and reviews and notes from both sides are kept.

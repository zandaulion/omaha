# Screenshot upload order

These labels are internal guidance for Play Console. They are not baked into the
images, so the screenshots remain direct representations of the app and widget.

## Phone

1. `01-watchlist.png` — A watchlist-first view with the aggregate fundamental picture and company cards.
2. `02-review.png` — The review queue for companies that need attention.
3. `03-research-overview.png` — A company research overview with score and evidence.
4. `04-widget.png` — The medium home-screen widget with watchlist health and score changes.
5. `05-checklist.png` — The traffic-light fundamental checklist.
6. `06-dcf.png` — User-controlled valuation assumptions and scenarios.
7. `07-compare.png` — Side-by-side comparison across selected companies.

Suggested external captions if a future framed campaign is created:

1. See the fundamental picture at a glance
2. Know what needs your attention
3. Trace scores back to the evidence
4. Monitor watchlist health from your home screen
5. Review every pass, watch and fail
6. Test your own valuation assumptions
7. Compare companies side by side
